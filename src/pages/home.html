Trust you can see

Proof, not promises.

The things households worry about most, answered in the app itself.

Verified by Otterly

Photo ID, right to work, a background check and insurance are looked at before a cleaner can be booked.

Photos after each visit

Before and after, in the app, with location details removed.

Your key-safe code, kept safe

Stored encrypted and shown only to your cleaner, around the time of the visit.

You know who is coming

If your cleaner asks another verified cleaner to cover, you see who and can say no thanks.

Reviews written freely

Held back for 14 days before they show. A cleaner is "New to Otterly" until they have five.

A marketplace, not an agency

Cleaners are self-employed. They set their rate, choose their days and choose who they work with.
