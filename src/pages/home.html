Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

Sign up for free and browse cleaners near you straight away. Message the ones you like, book a trial clean at their rate, then agree an ongoing arrangement with whoever suits. Payments, checks, insurance and the app come from us.

Who does my cleaner work for?

Themselves. Cleaners on Homekeep are self employed and run their own businesses. You agree the hours and the rate with them directly, which means no payroll to run, no National Insurance and no pension duties for you. Homekeep provides the platform, the verification and the payments.

How much does it cost?

Cleaners set their own rates, typically between £18.95 and £24.00 an hour, and you see the rate before you message anyone. Our service fee is already included in it. There is a single £99 registration fee when you first book, covering the enhanced background check and account setup.Cleaners set their own rates and you see the rate before you message anyone. Our service fee is already included in it.

Will my cleaner live near me?

Short journeys mean better timekeeping and far fewer cancellations, so we show you the closest cleaners first and you choose from them.

How do you check cleaners?

Everyone goes through identity and right to work checks and an interview before their profile goes live. When you book, we run an enhanced background check and take up two references. Cleaners can also take our free training on products, surfaces and safety, and every household rates them after every visit.

Do I need to be home?

No. Around three quarters of our customers are out during the clean. You can agree keys, a key safe or a smart lock with your cleaner, and the details sit in the app so nobody is guessing.

What are the photos for?

Your cleaner takes a few before and after photos of the areas they worked on, so you can see what was done without being there. You choose which rooms are photographed and you can turn photos off entirely.

How do I know how long my cleaner was there?

Cleaners check in and out on site and the times are recorded against your visit. You are only charged for the hours agreed.

What happens if my cleaner is ill or on holiday?

Your cleaner can offer the visit to another verified cleaner nearby, who arrives with your notes and preferences already in front of them. If they cannot, tell us and we will help you find someone. You are never charged for a visit that does not happen.

What if something gets broken?

Every cleaner on the platform holds public liability insurance arranged through our scheme. Report it within seven days and we will help you through the claim.
