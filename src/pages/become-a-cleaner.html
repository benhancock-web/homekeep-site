Cleaner FAQs

Do I work for Homekeep?

No. You are self employed and running your own business. You set your rate, choose your customers and decide your hours. Homekeep introduces you to households, handles the payments and gives you the app. You are responsible for your own tax and National Insurance.

What can I earn?

That is up to you. Cleaners on the platform typically charge between £18.95 and £24.00 an hour depending on experience and area. Our service fee is deducted from that and shown clearly before you accept any job.

Do I need to register with HMRC?

Yes. Anyone working for themselves needs to register as self employed and file a tax return. It is straightforward, it is free, and the app keeps the records you will need. We are not accountants, so ask one if your situation is complicated.

Do I need my own equipment?

That is between you and the household. Most want you to use their vacuum and products. If you prefer to bring your own, say so on your profile and price for it.

Do I need a car?

No. Plenty of cleaners here work on foot or by bus. If you drive, say so, because it opens up more work and your mileage between jobs is recorded for you.

Do I need experience?

Some, yes. Most cleaners here come from private homes, hotels or commercial cleaning. What matters more is references from people you have cleaned for.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can use WhatsApp you can use our app. Everything is a tap, and you can read it in your own language.

Can I work for other companies too?

Yes. There is no exclusivity. Keep your private customers, use other platforms and take work wherever you like.

What if I am away or unwell?

Block out the time in your diary and offer your visits to another verified cleaner nearby so your households are looked after, then pick up where you left off when you are back.
