We are not an agency. Nobody who cleans through Homekeep works for us, and we do not send you whoever is free. Homekeep is a marketplace: cleaners run their own businesses, set their own prices and choose their own customers, and we provide the checks, the technology and the payments that make it work for both sides.

We do not charge a large upfront fee. Our fee sits inside the rate you see, so if the arrangement does not work, we do not earn.

What happens if my cleaner is ill or on holiday?

Your cleaner can offer the visit to another verified cleaner nearby, who arrives with your notes and preferences already in front of them. If they cannot, tell us and we will help you find someone. You are never charged for a visit that does not happen.

What if it is not working out?

Tell us. If the fit is wrong we will help you find someone else at no cost. You are not locked into a contract.

Can I change my hours or cancel?

Yes. Change the days, add hours or pause while you are away, all in the app. Ending an arrangement is a matter of the notice you agreed with your cleaner, which is set out in your terms of engagement.
