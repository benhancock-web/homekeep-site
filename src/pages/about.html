We started this because finding a cleaner is harder than it should be

Ask around and everyone has the same story. A recommendation from a neighbour, someone who is brilliant for six months, then a message that they are moving away. Then it starts again.

Agencies solved half of it. You get someone, but rarely the same someone, and rarely at a rate that keeps good cleaners in the job.

Homekeep is built differently. We are a marketplace, not an agency. Cleaners run their own businesses, set their own prices and choose their own customers. Households browse, pick the person they want and book directly. We provide the verification, the payments, the insurance scheme and the technology that makes both sides work.

Our fee sits inside the rate, so we only earn when a booking happens. That keeps us focused on matching well rather than matching fast.
