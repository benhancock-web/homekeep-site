About Otterly

A marketplace, not an agency.

Otterly brings households and independent, self-employed cleaners together, and gives each cleaner one calm app to run their round.

Why it exists

Cleaners had almost nowhere to turn.

Households already have several ways to find a cleaner. Cleaners have almost nowhere that gives them regular local work and takes the admin off them. So the cleaner's app is the heart of Otterly, not an extra.

For households, that means a cleaner who is organised, verified and easy to reach, with every visit visible in the app.

What we believe

Three things we prove, not just claim.

Trust

Verified cleaners, photos after each visit, and a named cover cleaner the household can say no to.

Calm

One clear next step on every screen. Nothing to learn, no instructions needed.

Fairness

Cleaners set their own rate and their own days. Households see the cleaner's real rate.
