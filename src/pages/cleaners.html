Questions

Good to know.

Would I be employed by Otterly?

No. You stay self-employed and run your own business. Otterly is a marketplace that introduces you to households and gives you an app to run your round.

Who sets my rate?

You do, and you can change it whenever you like. Households see your rate on your profile before they message you.

Can I turn jobs down?

Yes, any job, for any reason. Turning a job down makes no difference to you.

Can I keep my own clients?

Yes. There's no exclusivity. Work with households on Otterly and anyone else you like.

What does Otterly cost me?

Otterly takes a fee from each visit. It's shown in full on every job offer and every invoice, so you always see what the household is charged, the fee and what reaches you before you accept.

How do my earnings reach me?

Straight to your bank account through Stripe, once card payments are switched on. That part is built and in testing now.

What if I can't make a visit?

Offer it to a cleaner you know, or to any verified cleaner on Otterly. The household is told who is coming and can say no thanks. You don't need approval from Otterly.

English isn't my first language. Is that a problem?

No. The app uses short, plain English. Message translation between you and households is built and in testing, and join pages in Polish, Romanian, Portuguese and Spanish are planned.

When can I start?

Otterly is in private testing in Hertfordshire and Essex. Register your interest and we'll be in touch as it opens.
