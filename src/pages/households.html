Questions

Good to know.

Is Otterly a cleaning agency?

No. Otterly is a marketplace. Cleaners on Otterly are self-employed and run their own businesses. You choose your cleaner, and the arrangement is between the two of you. Otterly provides the app that brings you together and handles the admin.

How much does a cleaner cost?

Each cleaner sets their own hourly rate, and you see it on their profile before you message them. Otterly's fee is already inside that rate, so nothing is added on top.

Can I try a cleaner before committing?

Yes. Book a single trial clean at the cleaner's rate. If you're both happy, you agree the days, the hours and the rate between you.

What happens if my cleaner can't make a visit?

Your cleaner can ask another verified cleaner on Otterly to cover. The app tells you who it is and gives you until a set time to say no thanks. If you're happy, you don't need to do anything: the cover cleaner comes.

How does my cleaner get in?

You add how to get in once. If you use a key safe, the code is stored encrypted and shown only to your cleaner, shortly before the visit. Each time it's shown is recorded.

How do payments work?

Card and phone-wallet payments in the app are built and in testing. They switch on once final legal and banking checks are complete. Until then, nothing is charged through Otterly.

Where is Otterly available?

Hertfordshire and Essex first, then further afield.

When can I download the app?

Otterly is in private testing and is not in the App Store or Google Play yet. Register your interest and we'll tell you when it opens near you.

I don't have a smartphone. Can I still use Otterly?

Yes. A web version for computers and tablets opens alongside the app. Use Otterly on the web.
