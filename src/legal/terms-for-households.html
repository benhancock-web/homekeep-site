1. What Homekeep is and is not

Homekeep is a technology platform. We do not employ cleaners and we do not provide cleaning services. When you book a cleaner through Homekeep, the cleaning contract is between you and that cleaner. Homekeep is not a party to it.

Where Homekeep collects payment on a cleaner's behalf, it does so only as the cleaner's agent for that payment and for nothing else.

2. Your account

You can browse cleaners without an account. Messaging and booking need a free account. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details private and for what happens under your account.

3. Booking and the arrangement

A trial visit is booked at the cleaner's own rate. If you both want to continue, you and your cleaner agree the days, the hours, the rate and the notice period between you, and those terms are set out in your terms of engagement in the app.

4. Your home and access details

You supply the access details for your home and confirm you have the right to. We do not verify them. Keep codes up to date. We are not responsible for lockouts, false alarms or call-out charges that follow from details that were wrong or out of date.

5. Visit photographs

Photographs of your home are taken at your request and you can turn them off at any time. They are personal data and the privacy policy explains how they are kept and for how long. No photographs of people.
