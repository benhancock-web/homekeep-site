1. Who we are

Homekeep is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact details are on the contact page.

2. What we collect

Account details (name, email, phone). For cleaners: identity, right to work and background check results, references, insurance documents, rates and availability, and the public profile you choose to publish. For households: your property details, access notes and any access code you choose to store (stored encrypted; only the cleaner on a booked visit can see it, only around the visit, and every reveal is logged so you can see it). Visit records: check in and check out times, and visit photographs if you turn them on. Messages between you and the people you work with, including machine translations.

3. Why we use it and on what basis

To run the marketplace (contract), to carry out the checks cleaners agree to (contract and legitimate interests), to keep both sides safe and resolve disputes (legitimate interests), and to meet legal duties. Photographs of the inside of a home are taken at the household's request and consent can be withdrawn at any time.

4. Who we share it with

The other party to a booking sees what they need for the visit. Providers that process on our behalf: hosting and database (Supabase), payment processing, identity and background checking, and the translation of messages. We do not sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Visit photographs: three months from capture, then deleted, unless attached to an open claim. Access code reveal logs: the life of the engagement plus twelve months. Account and financial records: as long as your account is open and then for the period the law requires. Full retention periods will be listed here.

6. Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection. To delete your account and data, see Delete your account. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Cookies

See the cookie policy. This website sets no tracking cookies.
