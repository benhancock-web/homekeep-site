1. Our position

Every cleaner on Homekeep is self employed, sets their own rate and chooses their own work. Nobody can be made to work through the platform, and nobody's earnings are held back by us. Identity and right to work are verified before a profile goes live, and payments go only to an account in the cleaner's own name.

2. What we watch for

Several profiles sharing one bank account, one phone or one address; a cleaner who cannot speak for themselves in an interview; rates set far below what others charge. Any of these pauses the profile until we have spoken to the person directly.

3. Reporting a concern

If you think someone working through Homekeep is being controlled or exploited, tell us through the contact page, or call the Modern Slavery and Exploitation Helpline on 08000 121 700. In an emergency call 999.

4. Statutory status

A statement under section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015 is required only above a turnover threshold. This page states our position regardless and will be signed by a director once the company is registered.
