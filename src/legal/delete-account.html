1. Ask us to delete your account

Email {{contact_email}} from the address on your Homekeep account with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm within two working days and you do not need to give a reason.

2. In the app

From the first public release of the app: open Account (households) or Profile (cleaners), choose Delete my account and confirm. Until that release the email route above is the one to use.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: your profile, your property details and access notes, any stored access code, your messages, your visit photographs and your device's offline copy. Kept only where the law requires it and only for as long as it requires: records of payments and invoices (tax law) and, for cleaners, the fact that a verification check was carried out and its date. Reviews you left about others stay, with your name removed.

4. Timing

Your data is removed from live systems within 30 days of the request and from backups within 90 days.
