1. What Homekeep is and is not

Homekeep is a technology platform. We do not employ cleaners and we do not provide cleaning services. You are self employed and run your own business. The cleaning contract is between you and the household; Homekeep is not a party to it.

2. Running your business on Homekeep

You set your own rate and change it when you like. You choose which work to take and which to decline, with no penalty for declining. You may substitute another verified cleaner for a visit without asking us. You may keep your own customers and work through other platforms. You decide how the work is done.

3. Tax and National Insurance

You are responsible for registering with HMRC, for your own tax and National Insurance, and for what you file and when. The diary, earnings, expenses and year end summary are record keeping tools provided as a convenience. They are not accounting, tax, financial or legal advice, and we make no promise that they are complete or fit for filing. Keep your own records; self employed records generally need keeping for at least five years after the filing deadline.

4. Self billing

Invoices are raised in your name from your logged hours under a self billing agreement you sign when you join and renew on a cycle. You remain responsible for the accuracy of your invoices and for your own VAT position if you register.

5. Time, mileage, location and work records

Check in and check out record app activity, not attendance or work performed. Mileage is an estimate from job addresses, not a substitute for your own record. Your work record is a factual statement of platform activity, not a reference or a certificate of competence. Check in and out is not a monitored alarm or a personal safety device. In an emergency call 999.

6. Verification and documents

Checks confirm what the records showed on the date they were carried out. You must tell us about changes. Document reminders are a courtesy; keeping your documents current is your responsibility.

7. Payments

How money reaches you, the service fee, timing, and what happens when a household's payment fails. This section is waiting on the regulatory position on payments before it is written.

8. Insurance

Public liability cover is available through an arranged scheme and is optional. We arrange cover; we do not insure anyone.
