1. What this website stores on your device

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. That is why there is no cookie banner.

The only thing it stores is the last postcode you searched, saved in your browser's own storage so the search box remembers it. It never leaves your device and you can clear it by clearing your browser data.

2. The app

The Homekeep app keeps you signed in and keeps a copy of your diary on your device so it works with no signal. It does not use advertising trackers. Details of what the app stores are in the privacy policy.

3. If this changes

If we ever add anything that needs your consent, we will ask for it first and update this page.
