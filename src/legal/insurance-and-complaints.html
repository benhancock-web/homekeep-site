1. Insurance

Public liability insurance is available to cleaners through a scheme Homekeep arranges. Homekeep arranges the cover; it does not insure anyone. What the scheme covers, what it excludes, the excess and who bears it will be set out here once the scheme is in place. It is not accidental damage cover for every eventuality and it is not a substitute for a household's own contents insurance.

2. If something is damaged

Tell your cleaner and tell us as soon as you can, through the app or the contact page. The claim window will be confirmed here once the scheme is in place. We will help you through the claim.

3. Complaints

If something has gone wrong with Homekeep itself, write to us using the contact page with your account email and what happened. We acknowledge within two working days and aim to give you a full answer within ten. If the issue is between you and your cleaner, the app's "Something is wrong" route is the quickest way to raise it, and we will help both sides.
